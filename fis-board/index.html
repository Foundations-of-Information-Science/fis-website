FIS POSTING RULES

– FIRST RULE: Maintaining the academic code of conduct; messages not abiding by it will be ignored. The FIS Moderator and the Secretariat will arbitrate in contentious cases.

– SECOND RULE: Participants have only right to a maximum of THREE MESSAGES PER WEEK (BUT JUST ONE OR TWO ARE RECOMMENDED).

– THIRD RULE: Clean posting; not dragging accumulated previous messages; addressing only to fis@listas.unizar.es, exclusively (a few other addresses might appear in the “cc”, but the lesser the better). Attached files are unwelcome.
