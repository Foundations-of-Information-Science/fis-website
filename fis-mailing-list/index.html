ACCESS TO THE MAILING LIST ARCHIVES

FIS conversations started in 1997. Recuperating the whole records herein –after several migrations and computer crashes– has been possible thanks to Xueshan Yan’s systematic compilation, and to the technical work of Dietrich Rordorf (MDPI). Nowadays the archive of messages posted to the FIS mailing list is available at:

  • 1997 to 2007: http://fis-mail.sciforum.net
  • Starting in February 2006 up to June 2018:  https://www.mail-archive.com/fis@listas.unizar.es/maillist.html
  • Starting in April 2014 up today:  http://listas.unizar.es/pipermail/fis/
